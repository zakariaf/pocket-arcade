## Tasks

### E25-T01 · Design notes, the Toybox design pass and the owner's one message

- **Goal:** Nothing is coded before the game is decided (new-game step 1; spec 13: "each game gets its own design pass before it is built"). The design notes answer every question of new-game-scaffold's "Before code" checklist for Swap Guard, with a proposed answer and its reason:
  - **What it is:** Telegraphed-attack tactics where the only move is swapping places, so enemies hit each other. Every enemy shows where it will strike; tap an enemy or a tile to swap places with it; then the strikes land.
  - **Classification:** turn-based: apply the move, then animate its events.
  - **Input:** a tap on the piece to swap with (`engine.panMode` `'none'`).
  - **Modes:** Levels + Daily (8.2; daily on by default; no Endless, so Home shows no Endless card).
  - **Packs:** 3 packs of 30 levels (D9), unlocked by stars (S8), with pack names in four languages.
  - **Stars:** the puzzle rule of spec 8.1: 3 stars at or under par, 2 at par + 2, 1 for finishing; S7 shows "7 moves – par 7" (base `s7-result-win`).
  - **Undo:** unlimited (a puzzle game, 8.5), never across a continue.
  - **Hints:** `solver`: the solver's next good move, highlighted on the board; 1 free a day, then a rewarded ad, unlimited with Premium (8.5, D2); `hints.freePerDay` 1, `hasHints` true, so S5 shows the hint key.
  - **Continue:** `once`: the last swap is undone; a rewarded ad, or free with Premium; offline and not Premium, none (8.10); while the ad loads the offer shows busy, and a hidden offer means none, so the result shows at once (L11).
  - **Ways of losing:** `swap-guard.lose.hit` ("A strike hit you") (S7: a short, friendly reason).
  - **Counters (2 to 4):** Enemies defeated (`enemies-defeated`, sum), Swaps made (`swaps-made`, sum), Double hits (`double-hits`, sum).
  - **Music:** none, so S6 and S11 drop the Music rows and parity uses the `--no-music` references (L1).
  - **Age rating:** cartoon or fantasy violence `INFREQUENT_OR_MILD` (step G1).
  - **Bands:** the proposed difficulty curve and the kill test's first payoff and twist (on the easiest level the first enemy is hit by another enemy within 1 to 3 swaps, and one swap makes two enemies hit each other at least once per run).
  - **The Toybox design pass:** Swap Guard is not in the Toybox design yet, so this task also draws it there: its palette in light and dark (a paint that passes the Toybox contrast rules), its logo on the 48-unit grid, its texts in four languages (name, tagline, goal, progress line, win title, lose reasons, counter labels, 3 to 5 how-to-play steps, the tutorial steps and three pack names), added to `design/toybox.html`, `design/toybox/tokens.json` and `design/shared/copy-deck.json` the way Line Siege, Flock Tilt and Scrap Shove are.

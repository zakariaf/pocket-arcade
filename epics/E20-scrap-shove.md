## What we will do

Make Scrap Shove a complete, shippable app on the one Shell, exactly as pocket-arcade-index's "Every new game" order says, each layer test-first and proven before anything depends on it:

- **The game, in one line:** Robots chase the player one step at a time and wreck themselves when they crash; shove the wreckage into their path. Step or shove, then every robot steps toward the player; crashes turn robots into scrap; clearing a floor brings a new one with more robots. The twist: scrap heaps are pushed like crates to block a robot or crush one behind the heap.
- **Its risks, from the idea hunt:** the twist barely showed in the toy (about 0.25 chances per game for a lookahead bot to crush a robot with scrap); the base is the 1976 game Chase; short runs.

## Tasks

### E20-T01 · Design notes and the owner's one message

- **Goal:** Nothing is coded before the game is decided (new-game step 1; spec 13: "each game gets its own design pass before it is built"). The design notes answer every question of new-game-scaffold's "Before code" checklist for Scrap Shove, with a proposed answer and its reason:
  - **What it is:** Robots chase the player one step at a time and wreck themselves when they crash; shove the wreckage into their path. Step or shove, then every robot steps toward the player; crashes turn robots into scrap; clearing a floor brings a new one with more robots. The twist: scrap heaps are pushed like crates to block a robot or crush one behind the heap.
  - **Classification:** turn-based: apply the move, then animate its events.
  - **Input:** a tap on a square next to the player to step there (or to shove the scrap on it), and a tap on the player to wait (`engine.panMode` `'none'`).
  - **Modes:** Levels + Daily (8.2; daily on by default; no Endless, so Home shows no Endless card).
  - **Packs:** 3 packs of 30 levels (D9), unlocked by stars (S8), with pack names in four languages.
  - **Stars:** the puzzle rule of spec 8.1: 3 stars at or under par, 2 at par + 2, 1 for finishing; S7 shows "7 moves – par 7" (base `s7-result-win`).
  - **Undo:** unlimited (a puzzle game, 8.5), never across a continue.
  - **Hints:** `solver`: the solver's next good move, highlighted on the board; 1 free a day, then a rewarded ad, unlimited with Premium (8.5, D2); `hints.freePerDay` 1, `hasHints` true, so S5 shows the hint key.
  - **Continue:** `once`: the step that let the robot reach the player is undone; a rewarded ad, or free with Premium; offline and not Premium, none (8.10); while the ad loads the offer shows busy, and a hidden offer means none, so the result shows at once (L11).
  - **Ways of losing:** `scrap-shove.lose.caught` ("A robot caught you") (S7: a short, friendly reason).
  - **Counters (2 to 4):** Robots scrapped (`robots-scrapped`, sum), Scrap shoved (`scrap-shoved`, sum), Biggest crash (`biggest-crash`, max).
  - **Music:** none, so S6 and S11 drop the Music rows and parity uses the `--no-music` references (L1).
  - **Age rating:** cartoon or fantasy violence `INFREQUENT_OR_MILD` (step G1).
  - **Bands:** the proposed difficulty curve and the kill test's first payoff and twist (on the easiest floor the first robot crash comes within 1 to 3 moves, and a shoved heap blocks or crushes a robot at least once per run).
  - **The design fix:** Strengthen the twist before anything else (spec 13: "the twist needs strengthening first"). Propose, measure with the bots and keep only what moves the kill test's twist number to at least one per run: floors generated with heaps in the robots' paths, heavy robots that only die by a crushing shove, and a scrap bonus in the score. The design notes name the measured numbers of each option.
  The owner approves it all in one message, which also asks for every per-game store step, each with the default that applies meanwhile.

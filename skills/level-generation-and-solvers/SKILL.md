# Level generation and solvers

Gives every Pocket Arcade game a shipped level table that is generated from seeds, proven winnable (by an exact solver at a recorded par, or, for score games that draw their future at random, by a witness bot), laid out in packs that unlock by stars, pinned by data goldens, and a daily level that is the same on every phone. A script regenerates the table from the game's plan and fails on any difference in values or bytes, a bad curve, an import outside the levels zone or a missing golden.

## Rules that must hold

1. **Levels are generated, never drawn:** a level is `{ level, seed, difficulty, stars }` and the board is `create(seed, difficulty)`, with level and daily difficulties on 0..99 (100 is the endless run). Why: spec 8.1 (same seed, same level); tables stay tiny and saves replay exactly.
2. **`pack-<n>.json` files are written only by `packages/tooling/src/levels/generate-levels.ts` from the game's `LevelPlan`, formatted by the repo's Prettier,** and the committed bytes equal what the plan generates. Never copy a pack from a template or an example: the pilot copies the Line Siege example's `levels/` code without its `pack-*.json` files, then generates the packs with `node --disable-warning=MODULE_TYPELESS_PACKAGE_JSON packages/tooling/src/levels/generate-levels.ts --app line-siege` (the output is byte-identical to the example's packs). Why: a hand-edited or copied table drifts from the generator; the checker regenerates and compares values and bytes.
3. **Every shipped level is proven winnable and its proof line is replayed through the real engine (`verifyLine`):** par games by the exact solver at the recorded par; score games that draw during play (Line Siege) by the witness solver, the game's greedy bot from a fixed bot seed. Why: spec 8.1 and 15 item 7, "no level is impossible"; a solver is never trusted on its own.
4. **Stars follow spec 8.1 through `starsFor`:** par games 3 at or under par, 2 up to par + 2, 1 for finishing; score games `[win, two, three]`, where `thresholds[0]` is the lowest score a win can have (0 when the win is not score-based) and a witness game's two stars sit at the witness score. Why: the result screen and the Levels grid show these stars to every player; one formula everywhere.
5. **The difficulty curve never falls, later packs are never easier, and no level after the third is a one-mover** (a witness game rejects levels its witness wins in fewer than its minimum, Line Siege 8). Why: players expect the game to rise; the owner approves the bands, `rate()` enforces them.
6. **The daily seed contract never changes:** `dailySeed('2026-09-26', 17) = 2599028541`, `dailySeed('2026-09-27', 17) = 2582250922`; each game's salt, daily difficulty and the daily boards of pinned dates stay fixed once shipped. Why: spec 8.3, every player gets the same daily level with no server.
7. **Data goldens pin the level table and at least three daily dates, with committed snapshots; they change only with `-u` on one file and a `Gate-Change:` trailer.** Why: a silent generator or tuning change would give players different boards under the same level numbers.
8. **Packs match `game.config.ts`** (`packCount`, `levelsPerPack`), open with 0 stars and unlock by half the earlier stars (0, 45, 90 by default); `levels.endless` is `{ kind: 'endless', difficulty: ENDLESS_DIFFICULTY }` exactly when `modes.endless` is true. Why: spec S8, no player stuck on one level; the config and the table can never disagree.
9. **Level code imports only `@e07/game-kit/**`, `@e07/<id>/rules/**` and `@e07/<id>/levels/**`, and follows the rules' determinism policy** (no `Math.random`, clocks, `**` or transcendental `Math`). A witness's evaluation lives in `rules/<id>-evaluate.ts`, never in `testing/`. Why: tooling, tests and phones must generate bit-identical levels, and check-boundaries enforces the same zone.

## Workflow

1. **Settle the tuning first, then read [references/levels-model.md](references/levels-model.md)** (types, the curve and the tuning rows, packs, stars, daily, endless). The game's balance sims (game-balance-and-bots) must pass before the packs are generated, because every level is `create(seed, difficulty)` on the current tuning; a tuning change after the packs exist regenerates them (step 5) and their goldens, committed with a `Gate-Change:` trailer. Get the owner's bands from the design notes: packs and levels per pack, minimum par per pack, star rule (par or score), daily difficulty; defaults are 3 x 30, minimum par 2/3/4, par stars, a daily difficulty of 40 to 50.
2. **Install the level kit once per repo.** First make sure fast-check is installed, because the kit's tests and the solver template import it and the bootstrap leaves it out (without it `tsc` and `eslint` fail with TS2307): when the root `devDependencies` do not pin it yet, run `npm install -D -E fast-check@4.10.2` at the repo root, then `npm approve-scripts --allow-scripts-pending` and, if it lists `fsevents` (the macOS file watcher of jest-haste-map), `npm approve-scripts fsevents`. Then run `node ${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/check-levels.mjs .`; for each `kit-file-missing` copy `templates/packages/<path>` to `packages/<path>` with its test (solver, levels with `witness-solver.ts`, `testing/render-cells.ts`, `tooling/src/levels/generate-levels.ts`). `dates/date-key.ts` and `dates/daily-seed.ts` are shared with the daily-and-statistics skill: copy them only when missing. The game's rules (with `contract/difficulty.ts`) must exist first (game-rules-engine).
3. **Write the solver test-first.** Read [references/solvers.md](references/solvers.md). A game whose whole future is fixed by `create` gets an exact search: copy `templates/apps/__GAME_ID__/src/levels/__GAME_ID__-solver.ts` and its test to `apps/<game-id>/src/levels/` (replace `__GAME_ID__`, `__GAME_PASCAL__`, `__GAME_CONST__`), choose the search state, move order and key, BFS or IDA*, and prove it with examples, the `verifyLine` property and a budget test. A game that draws during play uses `createWitnessSolver` with its greedy bot on `rules/<id>-evaluate.ts` and a fixed bot seed: imitate [examples/line-siege/levels/line-siege-solver.ts](examples/line-siege/levels/line-siege-solver.ts) and its test.
4. **Write the level plan test-first.** Read [references/generators-and-plans.md](references/generators-and-plans.md). Copy `__GAME_ID__-level-plan.ts` and its test: packs, curve (`difficultyFor` from 0 at level 1 to its cap, 99 unless the solver can only prove easier levels), budget, and `rate({ start, par, line, final })` with the owner's bands (a witness game: [examples/line-siege/levels/line-siege-level-plan.ts](examples/line-siege/levels/line-siege-level-plan.ts), score thresholds from `final.score`).
5. **Generate the tables (copy the generator and the plan, never packs):** `node --disable-warning=MODULE_TYPELESS_PACKAGE_JSON packages/tooling/src/levels/generate-levels.ts --app <game-id>` writes `pack-<n>.json` through the repo's Prettier; fix any `level N: no candidate` by loosening the band or the curve there, then rerun until 0 failures. Then `node --disable-warning=MODULE_TYPELESS_PACKAGE_JSON packages/tooling/src/levels/generate-levels.ts --app <game-id> --check` and `npx prettier --check apps/<game-id>/src/levels` both exit 0. Commit exactly what it wrote.
6. **Assemble and prove the table.** Copy `__GAME_ID__-levels.ts` (the `LevelsSpec`: plan, packs, `toLevelEntries`, solver, daily salt and difficulty, endless; replace the template's salt `0x5446` with the game's own) and `__GAME_ID__-levels.test.ts` (runs `levelsContractProblems`); make `game.config.ts` `levels` and `modes` agree, and set `endless: { kind: 'endless', difficulty: ENDLESS_DIFFICULTY }` only when `modes.endless` is true. If the game has hints, wire `hints: { kind: 'solver', suggest }` in the rules (never for a witness); that makes the game fact `hasHints` true, so the Game screen (S5) shows its Hint key (lead decision L8; `hints: { kind: 'none' }`, as in Line Siege, shows none).
7. **Pin the goldens.** Read [references/goldens-and-quality.md](references/goldens-and-quality.md). Copy `__GAME_ID__-describe.ts` (with its test) and write `describeLevel(state)` for the game: the template's grid renderer suits a grid game; a game with lanes, pieces or a tray renders those too ([examples/line-siege/levels/line-siege-describe.ts](examples/line-siege/levels/line-siege-describe.ts)). Copy `__GAME_ID__-levels.golden.test.ts`, run `npx jest apps/<game-id>/src/levels/<game-id>-levels.golden.test.ts --selectProjects golden -u` once, open the `.snap.ios` and read the boards (compare with [examples/tap-flip-levels.golden.test.ts.snap.ios](examples/tap-flip-levels.golden.test.ts.snap.ios)), then `npx jest apps/<game-id>/src/levels --ci --selectProjects golden` passes.
8. **Format, lint, type-check, test:** `npx eslint --fix apps/<game-id>/src/levels`, `npx prettier --write apps/<game-id>/src/levels/*.ts`, `npx tsc --noEmit -p apps/<game-id>`, `npx tsc --noEmit -p packages/tooling`, then `npx jest apps/<game-id>/src/levels packages/game-kit/src/levels packages/game-kit/src/solver --ci --selectProjects unit --coverage --collectCoverageFrom='apps/<game-id>/src/levels/**/*.ts' --collectCoverageFrom='packages/game-kit/src/levels/**/*.ts' --collectCoverageFrom='packages/game-kit/src/solver/**/*.ts' --coverageThreshold='{}'` and read the table (95/95/95/90 for game-kit; paths first, `--selectProjects` reads every later word as a project name; only `npm run test:coverage` judges the thresholds), then `npx jest apps/<game-id>/src/levels --ci --selectProjects golden`.
9. **Run the check** `node ${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/check-levels.mjs . --game <game-id> --report`; fix every `FAIL` line (each names the file, the rule and the fix) and rerun until it prints `RESULT: PASS`. Put the `--report` pack lines in the owner report and commit tables and goldens with a `Gate-Change:` trailer.

## Definition of done

- [ ] The game's balance sims passed before the packs were generated (or the packs were regenerated after the last tuning change).
- [ ] The root `package.json` pins `fast-check` exactly in `devDependencies` (no `kit-test-dependency` line); `packages/game-kit/src/solver/`, `levels/` (with `witness-solver.ts`), `testing/render-cells.ts` and `dates/` are installed with their tests; `packages/tooling/src/levels/generate-levels.ts` exists.
- [ ] `apps/<game-id>/src/levels/` has the solver, the level plan, `describeLevel`, the `LevelsSpec`, one generated `pack-<n>.json` per pack, and their tests; `generate-levels.ts --app <game-id> --check` and `npx prettier --check apps/<game-id>/src/levels` exit 0.
- [ ] `<game-id>-levels.test.ts` runs `levelsContractProblems` with the solver and returns `[]` (every level proven by a replayed line, at its par for par levels; packs matching `game.config.ts`; endless at difficulty 100 exactly when `modes.endless` is on).
- [ ] The golden test pins the pack boundary levels and three daily dates, and its `.snap.ios` was read and committed with a `Gate-Change:` trailer.
- [ ] `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint --max-warnings 0`, `npx prettier --check` and `npx jest <paths> --ci --selectProjects unit` and `--selectProjects golden` pass for the touched workspaces.
- [ ] `node ${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/check-levels.mjs . --game <game-id>` prints `RESULT: PASS`

## Anti-patterns

- **Editing a pack file to "fix" one level.** The next regeneration undoes it and the checker fails `pack-stale`; change the plan or `create` and regenerate.
- **Raising a test's node budget until a level passes.** A level the solver cannot prove within the plan's budget should never have been accepted; regenerate or improve the solver's key and move order.
- **Treating `budget-exceeded` as unsolvable** (or the reverse). Only `unsolvable` means the whole space was searched.
- **A heuristic that overestimates "to be faster".** IDA* then returns a longer line than par and players can beat par; keep it admissible, or use 0.
- **Changing a game's daily salt, the daily difficulty or `dailySeed`.** Every player's daily level changes between versions; ask the owner first.
- **`jest -u` over a whole folder or in a script.** Goldens change one file at a time, after reading the new boards.
- **Using `Math.random` or the clock to pick candidate seeds.** Tables must regenerate identically everywhere; seeds come from `candidateSeed(gameId, level, attempt)`.
- **Rating stars in the Shell with ad-hoc numbers.** Stars come from the table's `StarRule` through `starsFor`.
- **Copying `pack-*.json` from an example, or formatting packs by hand or adding them to `.prettierignore`.** Their seeds belong to another plan, and `prettier --check` and `generate-levels --check` then disagree; copy the plan and the generator and run it.
- **`solver: null` or bot sims instead of a proof for a score game.** A game that draws at random proves each level with `createWitnessSolver`; a lost witness is `budget-exceeded`, a retry with the next candidate seed, never `unsolvable`.
- **Importing the bot from `testing/` into a level file.** It breaks the levels import zone (`pure-import`, and check-boundaries' `rules-pure`); keep the evaluation in `rules/<id>-evaluate.ts`.
- **Generating the packs before the tuning is settled.** Every later tuning change regenerates the packs and their goldens; run the balance sims first.

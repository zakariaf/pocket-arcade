# Example: the one message to the owner for a new game

Flock Tilt is set up as its own app and builds on the simulator with placeholder settings. Its app id is `io.applander.flocktilt`, like every game on the Applander domain, and its Premium is `io.applander.flocktilt.premium` at EUR 1.99 with Family Sharing off, as for Line Siege. To make it a real app I need four things from you; I keep working on the rules and levels meanwhile.

1. **App name and one age-rating answer (step G1, 1 minute).** I suggest the name "Flock Tilt" in all four languages. For the App Store age rating I answered "cartoon or fantasy violence: none" (a wolf chases sheep but nothing is ever hit on screen); Line Siege answers "infrequent or mild" because its blocks hit monsters. Reply "ok" or tell me otherwise.
2. **App Store Connect record (step G2, about 2 minutes).** Create a new app (iOS, that name, bundle id `io.applander.flocktilt`, SKU `flock-tilt`) and send me its Apple ID number. Until then the rate link stays off.
3. **App Privacy and the privacy link (step G3, about 5 minutes).** Same answers as Line Siege; I will send you the list when the record exists. Please also send me the privacy policy address and the support e-mail for Flock Tilt. Until then the app carries the placeholders `example.com` and `support@example.com`, which can never go to the App Store.
4. **AdMob (step G5, about 10 minutes).** Create the app and three ad units (banner, interstitial, rewarded) and send me the four ids. Until then test builds show Google's test ads only, and the store checks refuse the placeholder ids.

Nothing to decide about Premium: I create the product `io.applander.flocktilt.premium` at the EUR 1.99 price point with Family Sharing off, and you only check it in App Store Connect (step G4).

Owner steps (not blocking), whenever you like: play Flock Tilt on TestFlight once it is there, read the Persian and Sorani texts, and listen to the sound previews I send. Nothing waits for them.

What to look at: nothing yet. What I cannot check without you: the store record, the privacy link and real ads.

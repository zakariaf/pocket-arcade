# Example: the one message to the owner for a new game

Flock Tilt is set up as its own app and builds on the simulator with placeholder settings. To make it a real app I need five things from you; I keep working on the rules and levels meanwhile.

1. **App name, bundle id and one age-rating answer (step G1, 1 minute).** I suggest the name "Flock Tilt" in all four languages and the bundle id `com.<your-company>.flocktilt`. Reply "ok" or give me the id you want. Until then the app uses the placeholder `com.example.flocktilt`, which cannot go to the App Store. For the App Store age rating I answered "cartoon or fantasy violence: none" (a wolf chases sheep but nothing is ever hit on screen); Line Siege answers "infrequent or mild" because its blocks hit monsters. Reply "ok" or tell me otherwise.
2. **App Store Connect record (step G2, about 2 minutes).** Create a new app (iOS, that name and bundle id, SKU `flock-tilt`) and send me its Apple ID number. Until then the rate link stays off.
3. **App Privacy questionnaire (step G3, about 5 minutes).** Same answers as Line Siege; I will send you the list when the record exists.
4. **Premium price (steps G4 and P1).** Default: the EUR 1.99 tier with Family Sharing on, as for Line Siege. Reply "default" or give me another tier.
5. **AdMob (step G5, about 10 minutes).** Create the app and three ad units (banner, interstitial, rewarded) and send me the four ids. Until then test builds show Google's test ads only.

What to look at: nothing yet. What I cannot check without you: the store record and real ads.
